## Needs a decision from Daniel and Alexandra (not changed)

**Amounts and concentrations**
1. **Exp 5 Tollens' test:** Alex used 10 drops of sample and 2 mL of Tollens' reagent; the current D.1–D.2 say 5 drops and 3 mL (12 mL of fresh reagent per pair). Which did the stockroom trial use?
2. **Exp 12 casein, C.3:** Alex used **10%** acetic acid; the current lab uses **20%**, still with "you may need 2–3 mL" (about twice the acid). 20% acetic acid is also missing from Hazards.
3. **Exp 14 Part D:** Alex boiled two portions of one juice for **10 and 30 minutes** (report columns "Boiled 10 / 30 minutes"). The current lab boils one juice and one powder for **15 minutes**, so the effect of heating time is no longer measured. Keep the new design or go back?
4. **Exp 9 Part B bromine test:** Alex reused the A.5 tubes (10–15 drops of lipid) and added Br₂ until the color persisted or 20 drops. The current version uses fresh 1-drop samples, up to 50 drops, and ranks the samples. It is already flagged for a bench test.

**Safety-related (EHS)**
5. **Exp 3 Part D combustion:** Alex had students burn their own samples in the hood. The current lab is an instructor demonstration.
6. **Exp 3 Part E:** Alex had students hold tubes in sunlight and note the HBr odor. The current lab uses 5–10 minutes of light and moist blue litmus, with "do not smell the tubes".
7. **Exp 3 bromine skin first aid:** 10 minutes (Alex) vs 15 minutes (current, matching safety data sheets).
8. **Exp 9 triethanolamine:** Alex said to add and mix it in the fume hood. The current lab requires gloves only.

**Teaching choices**
9. **Exp 10 B.2:** Alex used the 0.15% salicylic acid tube as the USP pass/fail standard ("lighter = pure"). The current lab calls it a positive control and says the test cannot show the USP limit. The current wording is scientifically more accurate; confirm.
10. **Exp 2 Report Sheet B.5:** Alex's rows named the isomers (pentane, 2-methylbutane, 2,2-dimethylpropane). The current rows are blank so students name them. Also Alex's D.1–D.3 gave the kit colors for Cl, Br and I.
11. **Exp 1:** the Snatoms double- and triple-bond technique (flat sides out for a double bond, in for a triple) was removed when the kit wording was made generic on purpose. Add it back as a "for example, in Snatoms kits…" note?
12. **Exp 4 B:** Alex tested pH on the pure alcohol before adding water; the current lab tests the alcohol–water mixture.
13. **Exp 13 D.1:** Alex offered 1% Pb(NO₃)₂ or HgCl₂ as alternatives to AgNO₃; the current lab uses AgNO₃ only. Also, if the stock starch is buffered at pH 7, it pulls the Part C pH tubes toward 7.
14. **Pre-Lab content questions** that were dropped (Exps 2, 3, 4, 14) and Exp 12's hydrolysis equation and peptide name. Optional.

## So details don't get lost again

- Before editing a lab, keep the co-author's last version as the baseline, and compare after each round of edits, not only at the end. The two passes above can be rerun: `source/tools/compare_details.py` (automatic quantity and name check) plus a step-by-step read of the procedure, materials, report sheet and questions.
- Any amount, concentration, time, sample size or "weigh/record X" instruction that changes gets a line in the change log with the reason, so it is a decision rather than an accident.
