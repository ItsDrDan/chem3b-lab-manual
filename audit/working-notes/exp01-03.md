## 4. Science and calculations
- Combustion: CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O ✔; C₃H₈ + 5 O₂ → 3 CO₂ + 4 H₂O ✔. PL5: C₆H₁₂ + 9 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O (O: 18 = 12 + 6) ✔.
- Densities: cyclohexane 0.78, toluene 0.87 g/mL ✔.
- **Br₂ stoichiometry (assuming 1% w/v, 0.05 mL/drop):**
  - Br₂: 3.5 drops = 0.175 mL → 1.75 mg → 1.75/159.81 = 0.011 mmol.
  - Cyclohexene: 15 drops = 0.75 mL × 0.811 g/mL = 0.608 g → 7.4 mmol, so about 670× excess and immediate decolorization is expected.
  - Maximum HBr from cyclohexane = 0.011 mmol ≈ 0.27 mL of gas at 25 °C (24.5 L/mol). Small, so litmus detection is marginal. Needs bench test.
- **KMnO₄:** 15 drops 1% = 0.75 mL → 7.5 mg → 0.047 mmol. Cyclohexene 5 drops → 0.203 g → 2.47 mmol, a large excess, so a positive result is expected.
- **Fig 3 (p. 32, `fig3_3.png`):** the third row draws and labels "benzene ring (aromatic)", but the lab tests toluene and the row's note is about "the CH₃ group of toluene". The drawing should be toluene, or the label should say benzene with toluene mentioned separately.
