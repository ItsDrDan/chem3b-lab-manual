## Needs co-author/EHS decision

1. **Exp 12 D.2 ninhydrin solvent:** the 0.2% ninhydrin solvent is not stated. Exp 11 says its ninhydrin spray is "dissolved in a flammable alcohol solvent". If the Exp 12 solution is alcohol-based, it is heated in a boiling water bath on a hot plate, and the Hazards list has no flammable warning for it. Confirm the solvent; add a hazard line if it is flammable.
2. **Exp 12 hazards, 20% acetic acid (Part C):** not listed in Hazards. Likely skin/eye irritant at 20% (verify on SDS). Also, the whey (pH ~4, contains acetic acid) goes to the drain; confirm that route.
3. **Exp 12 B.5 AgNO₃ and chloride:** egg white (and tap water, if used for the 200 mL) contains chloride, so part of the white precipitate is AgCl, not denatured protein. Consider specifying distilled water for the albumin solution and/or a sentence explaining this. (Unchanged: would alter a reagent.)
4. **Exp 12 B.3 (10% NaOH):** dilute albumin + NaOH often shows little visible coagulation; consider a sentence saying a protein can be denatured without visibly precipitating (needs bench check).
5. **Exp 12 C.3:** "you may need 2–3 mL" of 20% acetic acid is roughly 10× the acid needed to bring 20 mL milk to pH ~4.6; dropwise addition limits overshoot. Bench check. The "warm the mixture gently for a few more minutes" step has no temperature limit.
6. **Exp 13 volumes don’t add up (13-1):** Getting Ready sets aside ~50 mL starch and ~40 mL amylase, but the procedure uses 4×4 + 3×4 + 4×4 + 3×4 = 56 mL starch (plus the A.1 reference drops) and 4 + 12 + 16 + 12 = 44 mL amylase. I added "refill from the stock bottle if you run low" but did not change the amounts. Suggest ~60 mL starch and ~50 mL amylase.
7. **Exp 13 commercial α-amylase:** may have a different temperature/pH optimum and may partly survive boiling; 10 drops of ethanol may show little inhibition (carried over from earlier audit; needs bench test).
8. **Exp 14 gloves and CH₂Cl₂:** standard nitrile gloves give poor protection against methylene chloride (breakthrough in minutes). EHS to confirm glove type or "change gloves immediately after contact".
9. **Exp 14 iodine solution concentration:** still not stated anywhere (stockroom sheet open item).
10. **Exp 15 developing chamber size:** Part C lists a 600-mL beaker and says it reuses the chamber from Exp 10, but Exp 10 uses a 400-mL beaker. With 30 mL solvent, a 600-mL beaker gives ~0.5 cm depth and a 400-mL beaker ~0.7 cm (both below the 1.5 cm origin). Pick one and make the two labs match.
11. **Exp 15 B.7 variations (1) and (2):** these need a second strawberry, a second bag, and a modified buffer (no detergent or no salt), but Part B materials list 1 strawberry and only the standard buffer, and the Instructor Notes do not say who prepares the modified buffers. Add the extra materials or have the stockroom prepare the two variant filtrates.
12. **Exp 15 waste:** the base solutions (some in very dilute HCl) go to non-halogenated organic waste; capillaries go to a "broken glass box" (a fifth destination beside the four containers). Confirm both with the house waste rule.
