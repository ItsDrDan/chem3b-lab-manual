## Technician readiness

Each working-notes file lists reagents with amounts, glassware, shared stations and advance setup for its labs (section 1, "Inventory"). Per-class consumption is not totaled, because the number of students per section and the stockroom batch sizes are not in the repository. The stockroom cross-check in `OER_MANUAL_NOTES.md` (2026-11-15) is still the best reference. Its "Tell the stockroom" list is still open: phenol removal, Exp 11 TLC supplies, Exp 15 iodine concentration, and the chemical-list corrections.

## Student-profile walkthrough (summary)

- **First-time organic lab student:** steps are numbered and almost all give quantities. The gaps that would stop this student were missing tube sizes (Exp 7 D, Exp 10 A, Exp 14 A/B/D) and the undefined unknown sets in Exps 5 and 9. The tube sizes are listed as P3 items and were left for the co-authors, since they depend on stockroom stock.
- **Allied-health student who struggles with symbolic structures:** the Appendix B–E reference sheets and the redrawn figures support this student well. The two Exp 2 and Exp 8 model steps that could not be done or were under-specified are noted above.
- **Student using print only:** every procedure works from paper. No essential step needs a phone, login, video or game. The optional online supplements are the textbook boxes, which are not required. Answer spaces that were missing (Exp 2 Q.5, Exp 13 Q.7) are fixed.
